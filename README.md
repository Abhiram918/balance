## Meet Balance

Balance brings your computer usage into focus, helping you see where your time goes and better understand your digital habits.

## Features

**Application usage**  
See which applications you use and how much time you spend in them.

**Activity insights**  
Explore your usage patterns to understand your computer habits.

**App limits and focus tools**  
Access tools designed to help you manage application usage and approach work more intentionally.

**System tray**  
Keep Balance accessible while you use your computer.
